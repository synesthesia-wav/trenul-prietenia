**Two rules that everything else serves** (see `PLAN.md` §2 and the reconciliation checklist §0):

1. **We never edit a driver's ELD.** We prepare corrections with source documents; the driver makes the entry and signs.
2. **We never propose a correction the documents do not support.** Disputes escalate to the carrier; requests to reconstruct hours that were not driven are refused in writing and logged.

**The single most important commercial fact:** the partners' 18-month inspection record is under NDA and cannot be opened, quoted, or published — so we have no track record of our own to show a buyer. We sell on process proof (the Health Check, the SLA, the evidence pack, the monthly report) and we generate our own outcome evidence from account one, by pulling every fleet's pre-engagement FMCSA baseline on day one.
