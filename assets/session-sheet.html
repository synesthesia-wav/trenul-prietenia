The six questions

1. When did you get your operating authority?
You already know. Ask it anyway — it opens the clock.
2. Do you know when your safety audit is?
The one that matters. If he does not know, that is the finding.
3. Who actually touches the logs when something goes wrong?
Owner / office manager / driver / nobody. If nobody — that is the product.
4. What did the last one cost you?
Fine, down time, the argument with the insurance company. A real number, not "a lot".
5. What do you pay today for anything that touches your logs, and to whom?
Behavioural price signal. Never offer a number first.
6. If I told you it was impossible — not unlikely — that you would lose your operating authority for falsified records, what would it be worth per month for that to not be a thing?
Then: and what is the most you would pay per month before you would rather handle it yourself?
