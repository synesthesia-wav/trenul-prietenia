The two dates that matter to you

Within 12 months
FMCSA must schedule a New Entrant Safety Audit once you have enough records to evaluate. Your authority was granted {{ADD_MONTH_YEAR}}.  49 CFR 385.307
45 or 60 days
If you fail that audit, FMCSA gives you a corrective action plan to file. File nothing, or file something they do not accept, and your USDOT registration is revoked. 45 days applies to passenger and placardable-hazardous carriers; 60 days to everyone else.  49 CFR 385.321
Every day
An Unsatisfactory safety rating means FMCSA finds you unfit, and the prohibition in 385.13 applies — including ineligibility for federal contracts. Keeping a truck on the road after a final Unsatisfactory rating is a separate offence each day.  49 CFR 385.13; 49 CFR Part 386 App. B

Part A — In the cab, right now

 ItemWhy it matters
ELD data-transfer instruction sheet How the driver produces and hands over his hours-of-service records to an officer. 395.22(h)(1)
ELD malfunction reporting instruction sheet Without it the driver does not know to notify you in writing within 24 hours, or to rebuild his log on paper. 395.22(h)(2); 395.34(a)
8 days of blank graph grids The paper a driver needs to reconstruct 24 hours plus the previous 7 days after a malfunction. 395.22(h)(3); 395.34(a)(2)
Driver's last 7 days of records, in the cab The driver must have his copy and be able to produce it while on duty. 395.8(k)(2)
Every logbook signed and certified by the driver An uncertified log is treated as no log. The driver certifies with "Agree" in the ELD after the final entry. 395.30(b); 395.8(f)(2)
Shipping document number in the log The number that ties the log to the bill of lading. Missing it is a form-and-manner finding. 395.24(c)(iii)
Unassigned driving time explained or assigned This is a carrier duty, not a driver duty. Either annotate why the time is unassigned or assign it to the right driver. 395.32(c)
Last ELD malfunction reported to you in writing, within 24 hours Failure to report is a weight-5 finding. 395.34(a)(1)
Current annual vehicle inspection report in the cab Every commercial vehicle inspected every 12 months, documentation on the vehicle. 396.17; Part 396 App. G
