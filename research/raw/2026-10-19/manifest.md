## Derivation notes

**5–75 power-unit band.** From the Pocket Guide 2024 size split (grade C restatement): 3–10 trucks = 168,507; 11–100 trucks = 56,715. Assuming a roughly even spread of the 3–10 bucket across its 8 sizes, 5–10 trucks ≈ 105,000–147,000 (the bucket is lumpy: mass concentrates at 3 and 4 trucks, so the upper end of the 5–10 sub-range is smaller). Assuming 11–75 trucks ≈ 65/90 of the 11–100 bucket ≈ 41,000, and that 76–100 holds ~11,000 larger fleets, the band totals **≈ 160,000–195,000 active carriers**. 200 trucks = 0.10–0.13%; 300 trucks = 0.15–0.19%. Recompute from the primary Pocket Guide PDF and the census file before this number is used externally.

**Documentation vs driving violation mix.** Counts taken from the live SMS violation summaries for DOT 73705 (378 HOS violations), DOT 74432 (225) and DOT 2348038 (21), which show the weight-1 documentation codes (395.24, 395.30B1-ELDDFR, 395.30B1, 395.30B2-ELDDFC24, 395.22H4-ELDBRODS, 395.24C2II-ELDTN, 395.24C2III) outnumbering the driving codes by count in every profile, and the weight-5 paperwork codes (395.8A1-HOSP, 395.8F01, 395.8K2-HOSRC, 395.32B, 395.34A1-ELDDFN) present in each. Carriers with high HOS violation counts are the better sample for this inference — a large carrier's mix is not a small fleet's mix, and the relevant question is whether paperwork codes outnumber driving codes, which holds in all three.

**Moldova loaded cost.** 15,000 MDL gross ≈ $857 at ~17.5 MDL/USD; +24% CNAS ≈ $1,063 loaded before the IT Park regime. If the 7% IT Park turnover tax applies, payroll contributions are replaced and the marginal cost of an additional operator falls toward gross pay.
